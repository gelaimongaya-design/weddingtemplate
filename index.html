House rules

Four things we hold to

01

We hold your chair fifteen minutes

After that the slot goes back on the board and someone else takes it. Message us if you are stuck in traffic and we will do what we can.

02

Same price, every chair

Senior or junior, the price on the board is the price you pay. Nobody is upsold on the way to the till.

03

The board online is the real board

We do not keep a secret list at the counter. If a slot shows here, it is free. If it does not, it is taken.

04

Cancel two hours ahead, owe nothing

No deposit, no fee, no awkward call. Later than that and we ask you to let us know so we can fill it.

Questions

Before you turn up

Can I pick my barber?

Yes. Book any slot, then message us the name and we will put you against that chair.

Do you take walk ins?

Only into gaps. Most days there are none after five.

How do I cancel?

Message the shop number. More than two hours ahead and there is nothing owed.

Card or cash?

Cash, card and GCash at the counter. Nothing is taken when you book.

Do you cut women's hair?

We cut short hair of any kind. For anything past the shoulder we will point you to the salon two doors down.
