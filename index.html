04

What to do once you walk back out

Most of the result happens in the week after, not in the hour you spend here.

1

Leave it alone for twelve hours

No makeup, no actives, no scrubbing. Warm water and a plain moisturiser, nothing else.

2

Sunscreen every morning for a week

Fresh skin burns faster than you expect. SPF 50, and again at noon if you are outside.

3

Expect a little flaking on day three

That is the peel doing its work. Do not pick at it. It settles by day five on its own.

4

Message us if anything stings past a day

Send a photo to the clinic number. Someone calls you back the same day, not the next.
