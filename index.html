Questions

Before you book

How many can play?

Padel is four, badminton is four, and the basketball court fits a full ten.

Can I hire rackets?

Yes, one hundred pesos each at the desk, and balls are fifty a tube.

What if I am late?

The court is held for the whole booking, but the clock starts at your slot.

Can I book the same slot every week?

Message us and we will block a standing slot for you off the public board.
